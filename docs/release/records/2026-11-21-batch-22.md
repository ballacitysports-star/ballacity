## CI

- Head `c2f6f6c3`: `Playwright` failed 7 OAuth onboarding specs. A new Google or Apple user landed on `/feed` instead of
  `/onboarding/account-type`. Cause, in this PR: the root sign-in listener returned early unless the signed-in identity
  changed, which also skipped the account-type check and the saved `next`. An OAuth return can arrive as a `SIGNED_IN`
  for the member the tab already knows.
- Head `fa2627d1` fixes it: only the cache reload waits for an identity change, and the sign-in follow-up runs on every
  `SIGNED_IN` again, as before. It also has a unit test. The three spec files passed 20 of 20 locally against the sandbox,
  and then all checks passed in CI, including the full `Playwright` suite and `E2E smoke` 30/30.
