## Route

The same route as batches 1 and 2:

- Each migration was applied through Lovable's database tool, exactly as on `main` (sha256 checked against `main`), as
  one transaction together with its `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations` row.
- Before each one, a read-only pre-check confirmed that its objects were absent, and that every object it replaces was
  identical to `main`.
- After each one, every function, policy and trigger it creates or replaces was compared (md5 of the definition) with a
  local replay of `main`. All matched.
