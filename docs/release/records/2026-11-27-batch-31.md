## Not closed

Recorded in the migration headers and the plan.

- **Ask limits:**
  - After the third ask, the only way back is an invitation.
  - The cooldown runs from the previous ask, not from the host's no.
  - Asks made before the migration are not counted (production had no removal records).
- **Players who were not removed:** the older row-cap gaps stay. A deleted request starts over, and a player may insert their
  own request with any `attempt_count`. This is an owner item.
- **Blocks:** a block declines and cancels nothing; only a blocked player's move from declined back to pending is refused.
- **Paid capacity:**
  - The host's own approval is still uncapped.
  - The game page's places count, the send-time `GAME_FULL`, `payment_claim_block`, PayPal and the join gate still count
    roster rows only.
  - Stale approved requests with no roster place count against the place until cleared. That includes a deleted account's
    request; whether to leave those out is an owner item.
- **Five realtime channels on tables outside the publication** (`notification_delivery_log`, `trainer_org_join_requests`,
  `subscriptions`) still get no live events. Adding those tables to the publication is a separate database decision.
- **Remaining unguarded writers on the game page:** `onSettingsSaved`, and the optimistic and rollback writers of host
  requests, are not ordered against reads in flight. This was already the case before #482.
