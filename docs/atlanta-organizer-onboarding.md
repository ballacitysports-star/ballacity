# Atlanta Organizer Onboarding Script

A 15-minute, in-person (or video) walkthrough for a new organizer. Goal: leave with their team or game live, an 18+ setting applied, and their first invite sent.

## Before you meet
- Know their sport, usual day/time and venue.
- Have your phone logged in to a test account so you can show the link preview logged out.

## The walkthrough

1. **Open (1 min).** "I'll set this up with you right now. By the end you'll have a link to send your group."
2. **Create their account (2 min).** They sign up with their own email. Pick the profile type that fits: Player to host a pickup game, Team / Coach to run a team.
3. **If they run pickup games (5 min).**
   - Tap Create, fill in sport, time, spot, price.
   - **Age requirement: choose "18+"**. This is the setting that lets new people preview the game from a shared link before signing up. "Any age" shows new people a sign-in page instead.
   - Post it. Open the game page and copy the link.
4. **If they run a team (5 min).**
   - Create the team, then open Edit team → **Details** step.
   - **Age groups you play with: select only "Adults (18+)"** and save. A team that includes youth or teens, or leaves this blank, shows a sign-in page to new visitors, not a preview.
5. **Check the link logged out (2 min).** Open the link in a private/incognito tab. They should see the game or team preview and a "Sign up" button. If they see "Sign in to see this game/team", go back and check the age setting.
6. **Send the first invite (3 min).** They paste the group-chat message from `atlanta-flyers.md` with their link, in their own chat, in front of you.
7. **Close (1 min).** "Tonight, tell people to RSVP in the app. I'll check in after the game."

## Do not
- Add or promote any youth or teen games or teams on public flyers or social posts.
- Post a host's exact address or name in public copy without their OK.
- Promise features that aren't live. If asked about payments or leagues, say you'll check.

## After the session
- Add them to your tracker: name, segment, cluster, sport, link, date onboarded.
- Follow up the next day: "How did it go? How many RSVPs?"
- Ask for two introductions to other organizers they know.

## What to track per organizer
| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Date onboarded | Measures pace against the 12-week targets |
| First invite sent (Y/N) | Activation signal |
| Players who joined from their link | Their reach |
| Games posted in week 1 and week 4 | Retention |
| Age setting correct (Y/N) | Preview works |
