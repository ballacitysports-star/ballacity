# BallaCity: First-City Launch Plan

**Goal:** a city with steady weekly games, 20–30 active organizers, and users who come back, before spending on any second city.

## Pick the city
Choose where you already have contacts: a rec league, a gym, a pickup court regular. Write down the city and the 2–3 sports you'll focus on. Stay narrow.

## Weeks 1–2: Seed supply (organizers)
- List 40 targets: team managers, rec/league coordinators, pickup hosts, trainers, gyms.
- Contact each in person or by DM. Offer to set up their team and import their roster for them.
- Target by end of week 2: **10 organizers live**, each with a team or recurring game posted.
- Seed the calendar so a new user sees games within their first minute. Empty = churn.

## Weeks 3–4: Bring in players
- Organizers invite their own rosters using the app's invite links. Provide a ready-to-paste group-chat message.
- Put QR-code flyers at the courts and gyms where organizers play: "Find tonight's game."
- Target by end of week 4: **20–30 organizers, 150+ players, 15+ games per week.**

## Weeks 5–8: Turn on the reel loop
- After every game, nudge adult players to make a highlight reel.
- Make sharing one tap, with a small BallaCity watermark and a profile link.
- Run an "end of month recap" for each active team.
- **Adults only for public sharing. Never promote family or youth clips.**

## Weeks 9–12: Prove it, then amplify
- If activation and retention hit the targets below, add small geo-targeted ads (adults and parents: "find pickup near you", "run your team in one app") and partner with 2–3 local creators or coaches.
- If they don't, fix the product before spending.

## Youth and safety rules
- Market to parents, coaches and leagues, not to kids.
- Marketing messages go only to adults who opted in (broadcasts are enforced server-side).
- Lead with guardian controls and minor privacy limits in pitches to parents and leagues.

## Metrics (check weekly)
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Activation: new user joins a game or team within 7 days | 40%+ |
| Week-4 retention of activated users | 30%+ |
| Weekly active players in the city | Growing every week |
| Games created per week | 15+ by week 4 |
| Reels shared per 100 active players | Rising |
| Invites sent per organizer | 10+ |

Targets are starting guesses. Adjust them after the first four weeks of real data.

## Expansion rule
Open a second city only when the first has had 4 consecutive weeks of growth in weekly active players and activation is above target.
