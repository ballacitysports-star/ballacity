# Atlanta Flyers and QR Copy

Print-ready text. Replace `[ ]` items before printing. QR links must be tested on a phone first (see the checklist at the bottom).

## Flyer A: Players (courts, gyms, coffee shops near courts)

**Headline:** Find tonight's game in ATL.

**Subhead:** Pickup basketball near you. See who's running, when, and where. Free to join.

**Bullets:**
- Games posted by local hosts, updated daily
- Join in two taps, no group-chat digging
- Make an AI highlight reel from your best plays (adults 18+)

**Call to action:** Scan to see games near you.
**QR →** `https://[your-domain]/cities/atlanta-ga`

**Footer:** BallaCity. Where sports happen online. Adults 18+ for public listings.

## Flyer B: Organizers (league coordinators, hosts, gyms)

**Headline:** Run your team or pickup night in one app.

**Subhead:** Rosters, RSVPs, waivers and schedule in one place. We'll set it up for you, free.

**Bullets:**
- Post a game once and get join requests
- Track who's coming and who signed the waiver
- Share one link in your group chat; players join in a minute
- Hand every player an AI highlight reel (adults 18+)

**Call to action:** Text [name] at [phone] and we'll set up your first game this week.
**QR →** `https://[your-domain]/auth?next=/create`

**Footer:** BallaCity. Built in Atlanta.

## Short copy

**Instagram/DM bio line:** Find and run games in ATL. Link below.
**Group-chat invite (host pastes):** We're moving [run/team] to BallaCity so times and RSVPs stop getting buried. Join here: [game link]. Takes a minute.
**Text after meeting someone at a court:** Great meeting you at [court]. Here's the link to [game]: [link]. Reply if you want me to set up your run on the app.

## Rules for every piece
- Adults 18+ only in public copy. No photos or clips of minors.
- No guarantees about games being "free" or "open to all." Hosts set price and age limits.
- Don't print an individual host's name or exact address without their OK.

## Important: what shows to logged-out visitors
The public game preview now shows only games the host limited to **18+**. A game with no age limit shows a "Sign in to see this game" page instead. **Ask each host you onboard to set the minimum age to 18** when they post (Create game → age limit), or their link will look empty to new people.

## Pre-print checklist
1. Phone test, logged out: open a game link from an 18+ game. You should see the preview, not a sign-in wall.
2. Sign up as a new user from that link. You should land back on the game after setup.
3. Scan the Atlanta QR from a printed test sheet in daylight and in low light.
4. Confirm `/cities/atlanta-ga` lists at least a few real upcoming games. If it's empty, seed games first.
5. Replace every `[ ]` placeholder and the domain.
