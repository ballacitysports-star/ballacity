# BallaCity: Atlanta Launch Playbook

Applies `launch-plan-first-city.md` to Atlanta. Names below are categories to research and verify, not confirmed contacts.

## Focus (start narrow)
- **Primary sport:** basketball (pickup + adult rec leagues). Add a second sport only after 15+ weekly games.
- **Secondary, once stable:** adult flag football and soccer.
- **Geography:** do not launch metro-wide. Start with **2–3 clusters** and expand outward once each has weekly games:
  1. Intown: Midtown, Old Fourth Ward, Inman Park, Beltline corridor
  2. Westside / Downtown: Georgia Tech, Georgia State, West Midtown
  3. One suburban hub where you have personal contacts (e.g., Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Kennesaw)

## Who to recruit (research list, target 40, land 10 in weeks 1–2)
| Segment | Where to find them | Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| Adult rec league coordinators | Local league sites, Facebook groups, Instagram | "Rosters, RSVPs, waivers and schedule in one place; I'll set it up for you." |
| Pickup hosts | Recurring runs at rec centers, church gyms, outdoor courts, Beltline-area courts | "Post the game once, get join requests, no group-chat chaos." |
| Gyms and facilities | Rentable gyms, community centers, private courts | "Fill open court time with posted games." |
| Trainers and skills coaches | Instagram, local gym rosters | "Get discovered, fill clinics and tryouts." |
| Campus club and intramural leaders | Georgia Tech, Georgia State, Emory, Kennesaw State, others | Adults 18+ only, via the club organizers. |
| Youth coaches and AAU/club directors | Parent-facing, through the organization | Market to the coach/parent, never to kids. Lead with guardian controls. |

## Channels that fit Atlanta
- **Instagram and TikTok:** the city's hoops and creator scene lives here. Partner with 3–5 local hoopers and mini-creators for highlight reels, not big influencers.
- **In person:** flyers with a QR code ("Find tonight's game in ATL") at courts, gyms, rec centers, coffee shops near courts.
- **Group chats and Facebook groups:** get organizers to drop their BallaCity game link in their existing chats.
- **Events:** run 1–2 branded pickup nights or a small tournament in an intown cluster, with free reels for all adult participants.

Open a second cluster only after the first has 4 straight weeks of growth in weekly active players.

## Outreach script (DM)

I'd love to set up [league/run] for you for free and import your roster so it takes you five minutes. Can I show you?

## Group-chat invite (organizer pastes this)
> We're moving [team/run] to BallaCity so game times and RSVPs stop getting buried here. Join with this link: [link]. Takes a minute.

## Safety
- Public sharing and marketing apply to adults only. Never feature youth or family clips.
- For parent audiences, lead with guardian approval, minor privacy limits and team-manager roles.
