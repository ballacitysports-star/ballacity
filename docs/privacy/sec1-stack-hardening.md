### Open after round 4 (release blockers unless the owner decides otherwise)

1. **D-5: `hosted_games.host_id` correlates a minor's games for signed-in viewers.** Any signed-in account can list every game with the same `host_id`, so it can tell that several youth games share one organizer, though no longer who that is (names, rosters, ratings, posts, reels, teams and organizations are closed). Design: `REVOKE SELECT (host_id)` from `authenticated` on `hosted_games`, grant the remaining columns, and serve reads through a `security_invoker` view `hosted_games_public` (same RLS) that returns `CASE WHEN host_id = auth.uid() OR NOT private_sec.minor_host_hidden(host_id) THEN host_id END`, plus `is_my_game boolean`. Impact: 54 client `from("hosted_games")` call sites (18 of them server-side with the service role, unaffected), many with `select("*")`, which would fail with a column-privilege error; `src/lib/hosted-games-store.ts` maps `host_id` to `hostUserId` for "my game" checks, payments and rosters; policies that use `host_id` keep working (policies are not subject to column grants). Needs its own pull request with a full client sweep and the policy regression run. Not done here because a partial change breaks every game read.
2. Team and organization displays on a person's games are not detached automatically when their cohort becomes non-adult (only the snapshot is neutralized); the batch (with `_detach_unauthorized_display`) does it on request. Detaching a team from a game is a visible change for that team, so it stays a moderator or batch decision.
3. "Adult" is the self-declared cohort everywhere (an adult team owner or approving administrator could be a second account of the minor); no identity verification exists.
4. Organization creation by non-adults is still allowed when they host nothing; refusing it outright needs a product decision.
5. The historical batch has not been run anywhere but the test transaction. Production run: owner approval, then dry run, then batches.
6. Items in the table below that were not part of round 4 remain open.
